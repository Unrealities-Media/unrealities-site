    Release years are the earliest year in any region. The index records years, not months, so games inside a year are not in release order. Versions are tied together by a shared game id in the database, not by title, so renamed ports (Road Avenger, Revenge of the Ninja) sit with their originals and same-named different games stay apart. Compilation discs appear as a version of each game on them. Systems outside the index are listed under "Also on" when a game is opened.
