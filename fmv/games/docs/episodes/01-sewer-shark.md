### 2:00 Why this game exists (1 to 2 minutes)

The history, kept short. All from the index:

- The footage was shot in 1987 for Hasbro's NEMO, a games console that played VHS tapes, which was cancelled before release (later called Control-Vision).
- The video was directed by John Dykstra, the visual effects supervisor on Star Wars.
- Digital Pictures brought it to the Sega CD in 1992, and from 1993 it was the console's pack-in game. That is why it's the cheapest Sega CD game you can buy.
- The 3DO got it in 1994, in full colour.

*"So this is a game made for a VHS player that didn't exist, by the man who blew up the Death Star, released five years later on a CD add-on for a cartridge console. It's a miracle it's here at all. It's a bigger miracle I have to beat it."*
