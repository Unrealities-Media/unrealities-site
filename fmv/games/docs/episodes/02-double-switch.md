### 2:00 Why this game exists (1 to 2 minutes)

From the index:

- Digital Pictures' spiritual sequel to Night Trap, built around the same trap-the-intruders format.
- Directed by Mary Lambert, who directed Pet Sematary. Corey Haim plays Eddie, Debbie Harry plays Elizabeth, R. Lee Ermey plays Lyle.
- Reportedly Digital Pictures' best-selling game after Night Trap.
- It came back in 2018 as the 25th Anniversary Edition, now sold on Steam, Switch, PS4 and Xbox. Show a few seconds of it for the colour difference, with a caption saying which is which.

*"Night Trap got Congress involved. Double Switch got Corey Haim. I'll let you decide which is the bigger achievement."*

### 3:30 First attempt, blind (3 minutes)
