## Short answers

Buy a 3DO and nothing else. Season Zero runs entirely on the Sega CD tower you own. The 3DO holds the recommended version of 14 of the Titan top 50, so it is the one console worth adding. Your Windows PC, Switch 2 and PS5 cover the re-releases, and emulators cover the CD-i, Saturn and PlayStation. Updated after you said you have a Windows PC, a Switch 2 and a PS5.

- **Quarterhorse: no.** No emulator runs it (not MAME, Daphne or Hypseus) and there was never a home version. Only a few cabinets survive, all with collectors. The only footage route is a collector's machine, or licensed existing video.
- **Firefox: yes, in MAME only.** MAME's `firefox` driver is marked working (its source sets no not-working flags). You need a capture of the laserdisc as a CHD, which MAME does not ship, and an analog stick or flight stick stands in for the yoke. It has no home port and no re-release. In the index it rates Arcade, so a cabinet is the only other way.
- **Time Gal: yes, play the Steam remaster.** Taito's Time Gal HD Remaster (Steam, April 2025) is remastered from the original laserdisc. It has English text and a setting for the original arcade voices, so it is the arcade game in HD. The Switch versions are Japan-only. Record a short Sega CD segment ($32 loose) only if you want the port story: an English dub with some deaths cut.
- **Not every version.** The new field records one capture route per game, plus an optional second route for a comparison segment. The ports only need to be recorded when an episode is about the ports.

The rule behind every pick, in order:

1. Anything on Sega CD goes on the tower. A run on real hardware is the channel's angle.
2. A laserdisc original that has a store re-release goes to that re-release, because it carries the arcade video.
3. Otherwise, a store re-release (GOG, Steam, Switch).
4. Otherwise, the original disc in a named emulator.
5. Hypseus Singe or MAME for arcade-only laserdisc games.

The main sources: [Time Gal HD Remaster (Gematsu)](https://gematsu.com/2025/03/time-gal-hd-remaster-and-ninja-hayate-hd-remaster-coming-to-pc-on-april-10), [Time Gal releases (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_Gal), [MAME Firefox driver source](https://git.redump.net/hbmame/plain/src/mame/drivers/firefox.cpp), [MAME Testers on Firefox](https://mametesters.org/view.php?id=5681), [ScummVM ALG support, March 2026 (Time Extension)](https://www.timeextension.com/news/2026/03/fmv-western-mad-dog-mccree-now-playable-in-scummvm-alongside-six-other-titles), [D on GOG (Bloody Disgusting)](https://bloody-disgusting.com/news/3393242/cult-horror-classic-d-now-gog/).

**Hardware**

- **Buy a 3DO.** It holds the recommended version of 14 of the Titan top 50 and 20 of the top 100, mostly laserdisc and American Laser Games ports. It's also region-free. A Panasonic FZ-1 or FZ-10 both work: on PriceCharting a loose FZ-10 is $230 and a loose FZ-1 $211 to $268 (two listings), about $375 to $395 boxed. The GoldStar is the cheapest at $119 loose. The 3DO discs in the top 100 come to about $500 loose.
- **Already covered:** the Sega CD tower plays Season Zero and the 32X CD discs. The Windows PC covers Steam and GOG, including Time Gal. The Switch 2 runs the Switch editions of the Dragon's Lair Trilogy, Night Trap, Double Switch, Corpse Killer ; Mad Dog remaster sales on Switch/PS4 were not verified. The PS5 runs the PS4 editions.
- **Capture chain:** the RetroTINK and HDMI capture card are already owned. Identify their exact models and compatible video/audio cables before buying anything else; no cable type or output preset is established yet. Complete the short and sustained checks in the Season Zero briefs before recording a final attempt.
- **A CRT, only for real light guns.** The Justifier and GameGun don't work on flat screens. The pad works everywhere.
- **A Saturn is the next one to consider, not now.** It holds the recommended version of 4 of the top 100: Double Switch, Corpse Killer: Graveyard Edition, Policenauts and Enemy Zero ($162). A loose US Saturn is $160 (Model 2 $192), a Japanese one $100 to $116 and a PAL one $148. The Japanese discs need a modded or Japanese console, so a Japanese Saturn is also the cheapest way in. Mednafen covers it for free until then.
- **Emulate, don't buy:** PlayStation (the PS5 won't play PS1 discs, so use DuckStation), CD-i (MAME), Jaguar CD (BigPEmu) and LaserActive (ares). None of them holds an exclusive in the top 100 that emulation can't run.

## In the database now

[PR #29](https://github.com/Unrealities-Media/fmv-games/pull/29) gives each of the 607 games a `playToday` entry in its lineage, next to `best` and `titan`. Each entry holds the route, the record played, what to run it on, what to buy with the loose price, the reason, and an optional comparison route. The drawer shows it as a "To record it" line under How to play it.

- 35 games are hand-picked: the top 30, Season Zero, Firefox and Quarterhorse. The other games get a rule.
- Across all games, 356 are Emulation, 143 are Re-release, 89 are Original hardware and 19 are Not playable, counting the 3DO as a planned buy ([PR #30](https://github.com/Unrealities-Media/fmv-games/pull/30)) and the re-release sweep (13 more games found on sale; see [re-releases.md](re-releases.md)).
- Rule-picked routes are a sound starting point, not checked one by one. Fix any of them in `tools/capture/picks.json`.
- When you buy hardware, add it to `tools/capture/kit.json`. When you buy a disc, mark it owned in the tracker. Then re-run `python tools/play_today.py`. The rules are in [play-today.md](play-today.md).
