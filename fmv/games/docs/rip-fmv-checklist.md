# Pre-2000 FMV: eligibility checklist

Baseline reviewed **2026-10-28**. **188 unique eligible games**, of which **32 are explicitly selected for R.I.P. FMV**. Eligibility does not automatically select a game. Ports, remasters and compilations do not create duplicate game entries.

The [R.I.P. FMV section](../rip-fmv.html) shows the curated series selections. The [FMV Games Index](../index.html) remains the shared reference catalog across all eras. Editorial flags and reasons are maintained in [rip-fmv-series.json](../data/rip-fmv-series.json).

This is the agreed eligibility boundary applied to the current catalog, not a promise that every qualifying game worldwide has been found. Every quality level is included. Language, cost and capture readiness are separate production decisions; this list includes imports and is not an English-playable-only list.

The release classifications distinguish catalog-reviewed decisions from inferred/unreviewed facts. A catalog review reuses the existing descriptive research and its linked sources; it is not a claim that every link was newly verified or every edition played. QTEs and other gameplay remain unknown unless supported. The machine-readable baseline and every excluded/review family are in [rip-fmv-scope.json](../data/rip-fmv-scope.json).

## Boundary decisions

- Myst/Riven and other still-image puzzle adventures remain related history rather than default core obligations.
- Substantial real-time exploration/combat with video scenes is a hybrid; a high legacy FMV score does not override that distinction.
- Still-photo novels, non-game video discs and digitized character sprites do not qualify merely because they use filmed material.
- Prototype dates do not backdate modern releases. American Hero and restored unreleased projects are tracked separately from pre-2000 commercial releases.
- Consumer arcade ports qualify by their own presentation. Starblade’s video-based 3DO edition does not classify its real-time arcade edition or an unreviewed port.

## Maintenance

Edit `tools/classification/reviewed.json` for reviewed family defaults and edition exceptions; retain rationale and sources. Run `python3 tools/classify.py`, then `python3 tools/classify.py --check`, `python3 -m unittest discover -s tools/classification` and `node --test`. Run classification after older catalog importers. An evidence-backed scope revision updates this finite baseline explicitly.

The [recording rotation](fmv-play-order.md) supplies the opening episode order. The selection manifest controls membership of the separate series section; the eligibility list above is the wider candidate pool. The [classification reference](fmv-classification.md) defines all fields and uncertainty rules.
