# FMV coverage and purchase audit

Checked **October 25, 2026**. Release coverage is prioritized through **2024**; this is **not** a historical availability cutoff. Current reissues of older games count, even when the reissue arrived after 2024. Later games already catalogued remain, without a completeness claim for that period.

The catalog now has **1091 release records**, reconciled with the **1079** on current `main`. The original pass researched 24 additions: **12 were already added upstream**, so this PR adds the **12 still missing** and updates **86 existing records**. All 27 exact Steam products retain current US price evidence. Upstream keys, review scores and artwork were preserved; Titan scores, capture routes, re-release listings, the verification inventory and workbook were refreshed.

October 28 series classification: the [finite R.I.P. FMV checklist](rip-fmv-checklist.md) now governs pre-2000 episode eligibility. This earlier audit remains sourced coverage/purchase research for the broader catalog; its numeric FMV amounts do not decide the series boundary.

## What counts

Moving live-action, animation or pre-rendered video must supply the main playfield or principal narrative interaction: branching films, filmed investigations, laserdisc cartoons and shooters over video all qualify. A famous cast or an FMV tag does not establish this. Still-photo novels, conventional games with filmed cutscenes, real-time cinematic adventures and film-only streaming experiences do not qualify. Interview-led adventures may have maps, inventories or minigames; that does not automatically make them edge cases.

Cultural relevance is editorial: period importance, recognizable production or cast, awards, sustained genre attention or a meaningful regional audience. Regional Chinese and Korean successes were checked alongside familiar Western games. This is not a list of every product tagged FMV. The broader pre-existing catalog is retained, including adjacent titles; this audit does not relabel the entire collection as core FMV.

## Purchase corrections

- **Ripper / Black Dahlia:** removed unsupported Steam/GOG sale claims. GOG Dreamlist requests are not products. Original physical editions remain a possible secondhand route.
- **American Laser Games library / Brain Dead 13:** withdrew unsupported Steam/Switch/PS4 remaster claims across affected ports. Historical Digital Leisure, Wii, PS3, DVD and mobile editions remain distinct legacy releases; their existence is not evidence of current digital stock. These are explicitly unverified sales, not claims of worldwide unavailability.
- **Voyeur:** verified the exact DOS reissue on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1414500/). The CD-i and Macintosh records identify that as a different version.
- **Obsidian, Return to Zork, Lighthouse and Timelapse:** replaced incorrect or uncertain purchase text with actual product listings. These catalog corrections do not imply that slideshow/puzzle games pass the strict FMV scope test.
- **Kingdom II: Shadoan:** corrected stores to Steam/GOG. Its 2026 reissue is a valid current route for the older game.
- **Sherlock Holmes: Consulting Detective:** the three restored Zojoi cases belong to Volume I; removed unsupported reissue claims for Volumes II and III.
- **Time Gal / Revenge of the Ninja:** distinguish Sega CD discs from the arcade HD versions in Taito’s Switch collection and Steam releases.
- **At Dead of Night:** corrected the developer, format, play description and Windows route. You control Maya directly through a rendered hotel with filmed actors; it is not a remote-camera game.

## Scope decisions and unresolved availability

- **Plumbers Don’t Wear Ties:** retained as adjacent cultural history, but both FMV ratings were corrected from 10 to 1 because the game is predominantly still photographs. Its actual current PC store is GOG.
- **Wing Commander III/IV, Command & Conquer, Quantum Break and The Quiet Man:** their conventional play makes them adjacent to this strict audit despite notable filmed scenes. Existing records remain.
- **Myst/Riven, The 7th Guest, The 13th Doll and Yoomurjak’s Ring:** not used as required mostly-video coverage; puzzle play, still-image or real-time exploration makes the boundary debatable.
- **Attentat 1942 / Svoboda 1945:** included for their central filmed interview investigation. The developer’s teaching guide supports this format decision; no literal playtime percentage is claimed.
- **The Invisible Guardian, 428 and Root Letter: Last Answer:** predominantly photographic novels; omitted from core additions. Until Dawn, Detroit, The Quarry and Frank Stone use real-time 3D.
- **The Act / Hysteria Project 1–2 / DARKSTAR:** relevant historical candidates remain documented, but current authorized purchase routes are unconfirmed. This is an explicit residual gap rather than a false sale claim.
- **Super Seducer 3:** current Gamesplanet sale was found; retained as an adult-product exclusion under the repository’s existing scope. Super Seducer 2 fits the same filmed-choice format already represented by the original and was added.

## Evidence and verification

The [machine-readable audit](../tools/audit/2026-10-25.json) lists additions, affected keys, field changes, decisions and limits. [Exact current store checks](../tools/audit/store-checks.json) retain app IDs, titles, developer identities, dates, API URLs and US prices. The [modern](../tools/audit/modern.json), [classic](../tools/audit/classic.json) and [independent coverage](../tools/audit/coverage-review.json) research files retain primary source links and rejected candidates. New records are in `tools/pc/batchP.json`; the full 24-record research snapshot is `tools/audit/researched-records.json`. The earlier batches on main retain their own contents. Corrections are in each affected system’s `overrides.json`.

The current US price is for the base product unless stated otherwise. Complete-season bundles and DLC can cost more. Secondary-market stock and legacy console storefront checkout were not exhaustively verified. Cultural relevance has no finite authoritative master list, so this audit closes documented gaps without claiming a mathematically exhaustive global catalog.
