Catalog assessment: included
Deena supernaturally experiences 1666 through Sarah Fier's perspective while physically remaining in 1994; this is temporal perception rather than bodily travel.
Time-travel significance
When Deena reunites Sarah Fier's severed hand with her bones in 1994, she is pulled into 1666 and lives inside Sarah's body, experiencing the colony's witch panic and Sarah's execution from within; that section fills roughly the first 65 minutes. She returns to 1994 knowing the truth about the curse, and that knowledge drives the second half's plan to defeat its real author. Editorial ruling: an extended first-person experience of another era that shapes the rest of the story is scored the same way as Last Night in Soho and The Beast, rather than as a flashback, which lifts it from 0 to 7. It stops short of 8 because Deena cannot act on or change 1666 and the film never says whether her mind truly travels or she relives a vision.
