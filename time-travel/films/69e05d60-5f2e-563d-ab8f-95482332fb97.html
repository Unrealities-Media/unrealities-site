Why the archive makes this assessment (spoilers)

Fighting Brainiac, Barry Allen taps the Speed Force for the first time and lands in what he believes is a World War II battle in the past, where he joins Wonder Woman and the Justice Society against the Nazis while looking for a way home. The whole film plays in that 1940s setting, but partway through it is revealed that he has not gone back in time at all: he is on another world, Earth-2, guided there by Doctor Fate. The apparent trip to the past shapes the setup and Barry's fish-out-of-time role, but the reveal turns it into a dimensional crossing with no change to history, so the score drops from 5 to 4.

Public summaries call Flash's journey time travel, while detailed accounts identify an alternate Earth; consulted evidence does not firmly resolve whether temporal displacement accompanies the dimensional crossing.

How Titan works

Titan combines the archive’s quality, audience and legacy base with the significance of time travel. It is an index, not a score out of 100, and is distinct from the FMV archive’s scoring model.

Titan = base × [0.20 + 0.80 × (time-travel score ÷ 10)²]
