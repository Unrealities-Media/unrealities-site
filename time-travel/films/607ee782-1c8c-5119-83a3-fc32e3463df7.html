How Titan works

Titan combines the archive’s quality, audience and legacy base with the significance of time travel. It is an index, not a score out of 100, and is distinct from the FMV archive’s scoring model.

Titan = base × [0.20 + 0.80 × (time-travel score ÷ 10)²]

Catalog assessment: questionable
Ian dies and enters different existences; the sources establish repeated deaths but not clearly a resetting chronological interval.
Time-travel significance
Retain provisional 6/10. Ian is killed each day by the Harvesters and wakes in a different life without memory of the previous one. Reviews explicitly say he does not repeat days, so the reset is a reality-rewriting device whose temporal status remains ambiguous.
