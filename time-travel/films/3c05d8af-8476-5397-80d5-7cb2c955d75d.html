Why the archive makes this assessment (spoilers)

Retain provisional 8/10 for the extensive depicted reliving of adult life with historical foreknowledge. The experience is explicitly dream-framed, so this is not confirmation of literal travel and the related placement remains appropriate.

The relived life ends with Joe awakening in hospital, supporting a dream reading; TCM also presents it as transport into the past, so resolve the dream-versus-literal boundary before exclusion.

How Titan works

Titan combines the archive’s quality, audience and legacy base with the significance of time travel. It is an index, not a score out of 100, and is distinct from the FMV archive’s scoring model.

Titan = base × [0.20 + 0.80 × (time-travel score ÷ 10)²]

This film: 52.45 base × 71.2% weighting.

Partial inputs flag incomplete source metadata. Provisional weighting flags a time-travel assessment that remains uncertain; it receives no additional confidence penalty.
