How Titan works

Titan combines the archive’s quality, audience and legacy base with the significance of time travel. It is an index, not a score out of 100, and is distinct from the FMV archive’s scoring model.

Titan = base × [0.20 + 0.80 × (time-travel score ÷ 10)²]

Time-travel significance
Retain provisional 5/10, raised to medium confidence. Quantum physicist Robert d'Eglantine says a rift at 11.37pm will send his five guests back to May 1944 in occupied France, and once there they face troubling events. Reviews say the film deliberately leaves open whether the travel is real or an elaborate game, and that long stretches are uneventful.
