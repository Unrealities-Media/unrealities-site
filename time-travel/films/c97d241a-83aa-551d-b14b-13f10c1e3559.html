How Titan works

Titan combines the archive’s quality, audience and legacy base with the significance of time travel. It is an index, not a score out of 100, and is distinct from the FMV archive’s scoring model.

Titan = base × [0.20 + 0.80 × (time-travel score ÷ 10)²]

Catalog assessment: included
A curse makes Chuy consciously skip between successive Christmases without access to the intervening years. This qualifies under temporal-perception scope, consistently with Just Another Christmas; it is not a repeated-day loop.
Time-travel significance
Chuy skips successive years, discovers divorce, estrangement and his mother's death, then returns to the original Christmas after reconciling; the temporal progression structures the entire lesson.
