Catalog assessment: questionable
Possible futures, apparent foreknowledge, and cosmic time reversal coexist with an explicit imagination framing; evidence supports ambiguity rather than a definitive literal-travel classification.
Time-travel significance
Temporal structure drives the whole film. Nemo was born remembering the future, so the 118-year-old narrator in 2092 recalls every life that could follow his choice at the station as a nine-year-old, told out of order and revisited like replayed timelines. Arrow-of-time and entropy ideas recur throughout, and at his death time runs backward on screen. No one physically travels, and the film frames the lives as a child's foreseen or imagined possibilities, so it stops short of 9–10.
