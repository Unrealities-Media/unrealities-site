How Titan works

Titan combines the archive’s quality, audience and legacy base with the significance of time travel. It is an index, not a score out of 100, and is distinct from the FMV archive’s scoring model.

Catalog assessment: included
The original follows a river of time into prehistory; the cause is unexplained. The later American recut adds a dream frame, so version matters.
Time-travel significance
The boys’ river journey passes through successive prehistoric eras. Movement through geological time structures the adventure rather than merely depositing the characters in one historical setting.
