Alienoid

Choi Dong-hoon · 142 minutes

Gurus in the late Goryeo dynasty try to obtain a fabled, holy sword, and humans in 2022 hunt down an alien prisoner that is locked in a human's body. The two parties cross paths when a time-traveling portal opens up.

What kind of time-travel story is this?

Time travel drives much of the story.

Meets the archive’s time-travel criteria. The mechanism is classified as Mechanical.

Why the archive makes this assessment (spoilers)

The narrative intercuts modern and Goryeo eras; a time-jump rescue strands the aliens and Lee Ahn in the past for ten years.

Alien robots Guard and Thunder travel between eras; the Divine Blade is Guard’s power source and Thunder’s SUV carries the time travelers. Alien technology supplies the temporal mechanism within the mixed fantasy setting.

How Titan works

Titan combines the archive’s quality, audience and legacy base with the significance of time travel. It is an index, not a score out of 100, and is distinct from the FMV archive’s scoring model.

Titan = base × [0.20 + 0.80 × (time-travel score ÷ 10)²]

This film: 78.86 base × 71.2% weighting.

Partial inputs flag incomplete source metadata. Provisional weighting flags a time-travel assessment that remains uncertain; it receives no additional confidence penalty.
