How Titan works

Titan combines the archive’s quality, audience and legacy base with the significance of time travel. It is an index, not a score out of 100, and is distinct from the FMV archive’s scoring model.

Titan = base × [0.20 + 0.80 × (time-travel score ÷ 10)²]

Time-travel significance
Confirmed 8/10: the immortal Xane travels back in time to protect his past self Titus from Cain, the original vampire, who brainwashes Titus against his rescuers; in the climax Xane cannot change the course of events, Titus kills Cain and keeps the curse. Self-interference in the past and a fixed timeline drive the film.
