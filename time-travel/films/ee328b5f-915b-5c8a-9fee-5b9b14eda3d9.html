How Titan works

Titan combines the archive’s quality, audience and legacy base with the significance of time travel. It is an index, not a score out of 100, and is distinct from the FMV archive’s scoring model.

Titan = base × [0.20 + 0.80 × (time-travel score ÷ 10)²]

Time-travel significance
Retain provisional 5/10. An amnesiac man found on a Greek island beach after a storm, apparently a visitor from another time with a brother also lost in time, bonds with a widow and her son. Reviews describe a slow study of human nature with Christ symbolism, and the literal temporal mechanics stay unclear.
