Catalog assessment: included
The family perpetually repeats the same day to prevent a daughter's death; sourced synopsis describes a repeating interval.
Time-travel significance
The journalist Villafañe and the poet Oribe come to La Adela, a remote Pampas estate where the severe Vermehren lives cut off with his daughters. The secret the film uncovers is that Lucía has an incurable illness, so her father has decided to stop the passage of time by having the household repeat the same daily routine without the slightest change, as if the same day went on for over a year. When the outsiders break the ritual, Lucía dies and the mystery turns on Oribe's part in it. Halting time is the central idea, but it is a family's ritual whose fantastic success is left ambiguous rather than a depicted loop or journey, so the score drops from 7 to 5.
