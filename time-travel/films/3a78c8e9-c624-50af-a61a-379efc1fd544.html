Read the assessment (spoilers)
Catalog assessment: questionable
Sources disagree: Filmsite describes time reversal at the ending, while the full Wikipedia plot describes memory erasure and a next-day repetition only. Direct viewing is needed to distinguish actual temporal reset from repeated amnesia.
Time-travel significance
Jesse and Chester wake with no memory of the night before and spend the film piecing it together while cultists, aliens and a gang of space nerds chase them for the Continuum Transfunctioner. After they save the universe, the Nordic aliens take back the device and wipe the pair's memories, and they wake the next morning at home with the car at the curb, mirroring the opening. Most plot accounts describe that ending as a memory wipe and a repeated situation rather than a rewind of time, and nothing else in the film involves time, so it drops from 1 to 0.
