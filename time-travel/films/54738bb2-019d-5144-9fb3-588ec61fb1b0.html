The Girl From Tomorrow

Kathy Mueller · 104 minutes

Edited entirely from the TV show. A teenager from the year 3000 takes part in a time-travel experiment, but due to a malfunction finds herself sent back to the year 1990, along with a criminal from the year 2500. She must convince people of this time period that she is not crazy and that the criminal from the future must be stopped before he tries to take over everything.

How Titan works

Titan combines the archive’s quality, audience and legacy base with the significance of time travel. It is an index, not a score out of 100, and is distinct from the FMV archive’s scoring model.

Titan = base × [0.20 + 0.80 × (time-travel score ÷ 10)²]

This film: 38.34 base × 59.2% weighting.

Partial inputs flag incomplete source metadata. Provisional weighting flags a time-travel assessment that remains uncertain; it receives no additional confidence penalty.

Confirmed 7/10: the 1992 telemovie compiles all twelve episodes of series one, in which Alana from the year 3000 is taken hostage by Silverthorn from 2500 and stranded in 1990, where the Kelly family help her recover the time capsule.
