Her Blue Sky

Tatsuyuki Nagai · 108 minutes

High school student Aoi Aioi lives with her elder sister, Akane, after a tragic accident took their parents away 13 years ago. Because Akane has since been taking care of her single-handedly, Aoi wants to move to Tokyo after her graduation to relieve her sister's burden and pursue a musical career, inspired by Akane's ex-boyfriend Shinnosuke "Shinno" Kanamuro. Shinno was part of a band until he left for Tokyo to become a professional guitarist after the sisters' parents passed away, and he was never to be seen again.

Catalog assessment: included
A living spirit of Shinno's eighteen-year-old self appears thirteen years later alongside his adult self.
Time-travel significance
Thirteen years after Shinnosuke left town, his eighteen-year-old self, Shinno, appears in the storeroom where he used to practise, a living spirit out of the past who exists alongside his disillusioned adult self. Shinno's presence drives the relationships: Aoi falls for the boy her sister once loved, and his disappointment in who he became confronts the adult Shinnosuke and Akane with their old promise. The displaced past self is the film's central premise, but the film explores it emotionally rather than temporally: there is no travel between times, no change to the past and only a vague explanation of how he exists, so it stays at 6.
