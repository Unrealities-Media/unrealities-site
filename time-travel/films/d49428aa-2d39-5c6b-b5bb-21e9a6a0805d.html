Claire Parker is going to die. At the hands of a sadistic and depraved killer, she will endure a terrifying, unimaginable brutal death--and it will all happen again. After being beaten, dragged, sliced, and stabbed, Claire awakens at work--where it all began--untouched and unharmed. But the hellish ordeal is far from over. The madman is back and he's ready for more blood...

How Titan works

Titan combines the archive’s quality, audience and legacy base with the significance of time travel. It is an index, not a score out of 100, and is distinct from the FMV archive’s scoring model.

Titan = base × [0.20 + 0.80 × (time-travel score ÷ 10)²]

This film: 40.15 base × 71.2% weighting.

Partial inputs flag incomplete source metadata. Provisional weighting flags a time-travel assessment that remains uncertain; it receives no additional confidence penalty.

Catalog assessment: included
After being murdered, Claire awakens unharmed at the start and the ordeal recurs.
Time-travel significance
Retain provisional 8/10. Claire is murdered by the same killer and wakes again at the convenience store, in a horror variation on Groundhog Day that structures the film. The ending's explanation for the loop is still unsourced, so its supernatural framing cannot yet be weighed.
