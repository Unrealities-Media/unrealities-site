Time-travel significance
The middle chapter opens with the Flash gone after Part One's time-scattered ordeal and follows heroes gathered from many universes as they phase Earths out of the path of the antimatter wave, fight shadow demons and uncover the secrets of the Monitor, Psycho-Pirate and Supergirl. It is a multiverse story: the temporal content is limited to fallout from Barry's earlier unmooring in time and Kara's backstory of returning from the Legion's future era, which colours her character without driving events. That limited use keeps it at 2, between Part One's 8 and Part Three's 5.
