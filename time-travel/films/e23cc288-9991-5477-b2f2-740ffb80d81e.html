How Titan works

Titan combines the archive’s quality, audience and legacy base with the significance of time travel. It is an index, not a score out of 100, and is distinct from the FMV archive’s scoring model.

Titan = base × [0.20 + 0.80 × (time-travel score ÷ 10)²]

This film: 18.57 base × 27.2% weighting.

Partial inputs flag incomplete source metadata. Provisional weighting flags a time-travel assessment that remains uncertain; it receives no additional confidence penalty.

Catalog assessment: questionable
A dead inventor’s device links his children to the supernatural world; sources do not resolve whether this includes time displacement rather than contact with the dead.
Time-travel significance
Retain provisional 3/10. After inventor James Jones dies, his estranged children have days before the bank seizes the house and follow a scavenger hunt to his last invention, a link to the supernatural world that could save the souls of their father and brother. Reviews call it part drama, part ghost story, part science fiction and describe no time travel, so the score may need to fall once the ending is checked.
