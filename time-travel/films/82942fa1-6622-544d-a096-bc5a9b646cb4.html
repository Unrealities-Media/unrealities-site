Catalog assessment: questionable
The time-machine adventure is framed by a possible dream ending, so literal displacement remains uncertain.
Time-travel significance
Shurik's home-built time machine opens a portal to 16th-century Moscow and swaps the building manager Bunsha and the burglar Miloslavsky with Ivan the Terrible. The rest of the film cross-cuts the two stranded parties: Bunsha forced to play tsar at court, Ivan loose in 1970s Moscow, and the race to repair the machine and swap them back. Temporal displacement drives nearly every scene. It stops short of 9–10 because time is a setting for farce rather than something with paradoxes or lasting consequences, and the closing reveal frames the adventure as Shurik's dream.
