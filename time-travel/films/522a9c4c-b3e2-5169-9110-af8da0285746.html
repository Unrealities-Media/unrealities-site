Catalog assessment: included
A divine demonstration repeats the same kidnapping scenario at different starting times.
Time-travel significance
Retain provisional 8/10. Inspired by Run Lola Run, the kidnapping plot plays out in three variations that Lord Shiva presents to show how a one-minute delay changes a life. The alternative timelines structure the whole film, but they are a divine demonstration rather than lived repetition.
